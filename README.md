## My contribution in this fork

This repository is a personal fork of the official MsPASS tutorial materials.

My main work here was completed on the `raj-2025` branch, where I followed the 2025 EarthScope MsPASS course and worked through the hands-on materials and homework notebooks in `Earthscope2025/`, including `Homework1.ipynb` and `Homework2.ipynb`.

I use this repository as a record of my practical learning in:
- seismic-data management with MsPASS,
- metadata and MongoDB-based workflows,
- parallel processing,
- and cloud/S3-oriented seismic-data handling.
